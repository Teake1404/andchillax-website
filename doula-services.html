What is a postnatal doula?

A postnatal doula is a trained, non-medical professional who supports you in the first weeks home with your baby — feeding, recovery, sleep, and the emotional reality of life with a newborn. I focus on postnatal support only; I don't attend births. That means when I'm with you, I'm fully present.

I bring fifteen years of NHS postnatal-ward experience to that role, including a current part-time post as a Postnatal Midwifery Assistant & Infant Feeding Specialist. So you get clinical expertise on feeding and recovery, alongside the calm, practical support that makes those early weeks easier.
