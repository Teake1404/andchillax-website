What is a doula?

A doula is a trained, non-medical professional who provides emotional, physical and informational support to people and their partners through pregnancy, birth and the postnatal period. We don't replace your midwife or obstetrician — we complement them by being a continuous presence who knows you, knows your wishes, and stays with you when the clinical team has to rotate.

Research consistently shows that having a doula reduces the likelihood of medical interventions and improves how people feel about their birth experience. I bring fifteen years of NHS postnatal-ward experience to that role — which means I'm fluent in clinical reality as well as the emotional and practical side.
