FAQ

Is this Ofsted-compliant?

Yes — this is the Level 3 Emergency Paediatric First Aid qualification, Ofsted-recognised. For Ofsted Compulsory Register (childminders/nannies on EYFS), you need the 12-hour course instead.

Will my employer accept this?

Yes for most childcare-adjacent roles. If you're being asked specifically for "12-hour paediatric first aid" by an Ofsted-registered nursery, you'll need that course. Otherwise this is widely accepted.
