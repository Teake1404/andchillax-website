Who this course is for

Existing First Aid at Work qualified staff whose 3-year certificate is approaching expiry. Also suitable for staff at medium-risk workplaces who don't need the full 3-day course.

What you'll learn

A refresher and update of the full FAW curriculum:

  • Latest CPR & AED protocols
  • Major bleeds & shock management
  • Spinal injury, suspected fractures
  • Burns, scalds, poisoning
  • Heart attack, stroke, asthma, anaphylaxis
  • Practical assessment

How the hybrid format works

  1. Pre-course online module — ~3 hours, self-paced, covers theory and updates
  2. Day 1 classroom — full day at your venue, hands-on practical
  3. Day 2 morning — half-day refresher + practical assessment

Compressed compared to the 3-day course but covers all HSE requirements for Level 3 FAW renewal.
