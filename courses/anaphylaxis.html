Who this course is for

  • Parents of newly-diagnosed allergic children
  • Grandparents, family & childminders looking after allergic children
  • School staff managing students with allergies
  • Nursery teams (combined with paediatric course)
  • Workplaces with allergic staff or customers (food service)

What you'll learn

  • Recognising mild vs severe allergic reactions
  • Anaphylaxis — the symptoms that mean "act now"
  • How to use an EpiPen, Jext and Emerade auto-injector — practised on trainer pens
  • What to do AFTER you've used the EpiPen
  • Asthma attack management (often co-occurs with anaphylaxis)
  • When to call 999 vs 111
  • Building an allergy action plan
  • School / nursery medication storage and policy

Available dates

Open courses run roughly every 6 weeks in Anerley. Group bookings (schools, nurseries) usually scheduled within 2 weeks.
