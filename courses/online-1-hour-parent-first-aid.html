What we cover in 60 minutes

Tighter focus than the 2-hour in-person course — we hit the absolute essentials:

  • Infant CPR — demonstrated, then I check your technique on camera
  • Choking response — back blows + chest thrusts
  • Fever management — when to wait, when to call
  • Recovery position
  • Q&A — bring your specific worries

Who this is for

  • Parents who can't make an in-person class (work, travel, baby's nap schedule)
  • Parents needing a refresher between in-person classes
  • Grandparents who can't travel to South London
  • Parents outside London who still want my style of teaching

Want the full hands-on course instead?

If you're in South London, the 2-hour private course at home is honestly much better — practising on a real-feel manikin makes a huge difference vs watching someone else do it on Zoom. Online is for when in-person isn't an option.
