What you'll learn

The full Ofsted-compliant curriculum, designed for everyday early-years scenarios:

  • The role of the first aider and managing an emergency
  • Basic Life Support — CPR, primary & secondary surveys
  • Use of an AED (Automated External Defibrillator)
  • Allergic reactions, anaphylaxis & auto-injector use
  • Asthma — recognising and managing an attack
  • Wounds, bleeding, fractures, sprains, dislocations
  • Burns, choking, head injuries
  • Treatment of an unconscious casualty
  • Meningitis, diabetes, seizures, eye injuries
  • Anaemia and Sickle Cell awareness

How the blended learning works

This is a Level 3 Ofsted-compliant qualification delivered over two stages:

  1. 6 hours online — self-paced eLearning you can fit around your work and family. Covers theory, knowledge checks, and pre-classroom prep. Most candidates complete this across 3-5 sittings.
  2. 6 hours in classroom — one full day in our Anerley classroom (or at your nursery for group bookings). All hands-on practical: CPR on infant and child manikins, recovery position, choking response, AED use. You'll be assessed via practical observation.

By the end of the day, you'll receive your Ofsted-compliant certificate, valid for three years.

Frequently asked questions

Is this course Ofsted-compliant for the Childcare Register?

Yes — fully compliant for both the Compulsory Childcare Register and Voluntary Register. This is the full 12-hour Level 3 Award in Paediatric First Aid required for childminders, nursery staff and anyone working with children under 8 on the EYFS framework.

How long is the certificate valid?

Three years from the date of issue. I'll send you a calendar reminder 60 days before expiry so you can rebook in plenty of time.

Can I do all 12 hours online?

No — Ofsted requires at least 6 hours of in-person practical assessment. The online portion gives us more classroom time for hands-on practice rather than theory.

Do you come to my nursery for group bookings?

Yes, for groups of 8 or more. I cover all of South East and South West London. Group fee is £700 for up to 14 delegates, which usually works out cheaper per person than individual bookings.

What's your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 7 days before the course. Within 7 days, I can transfer your booking to another date or refund 50%. Sickness or genuine emergencies — just let me know, I'll always work something out.
