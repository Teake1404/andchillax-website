Who this course is for

Higher-risk workplaces or first-time appointed first aiders who need the full 3-day Level 3 qualification. Common industries:

  • Manufacturing & light industrial
  • Construction sites (smaller projects)
  • Laboratories & medical facilities
  • Larger nurseries (combined with paediatric component)
  • Workplaces with 50+ employees
  • Anywhere HSE risk assessment requires full FAW cover

What you'll learn

The complete HSE FAW curriculum (everything in the 1-day EFAW course, plus):

  • Major bleeds & shock management (advanced)
  • Spinal injuries & immobilisation
  • Suspected fractures & dislocations
  • Heart attack, stroke recognition
  • Asthma, anaphylaxis, diabetic emergencies
  • Severe burns & scalds
  • Eye injuries & chemical exposure
  • Multi-casualty incidents & triage basics
  • First aider responsibilities & record-keeping

How the format works

Day 0 (online, ~6 hours, self-paced): theory, knowledge checks, pre-classroom prep. Most learners do this across a week.

Day 1 (classroom, 7 hours): CPR, AED, choking, bleeding, recovery position. All hands-on.

Day 2 (classroom, 7 hours): Major incidents, fractures, advanced scenarios.

Day 3 (classroom, 5 hours + assessment): Practical assessment, written paper, certification.
