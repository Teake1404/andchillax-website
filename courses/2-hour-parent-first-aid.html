What you'll learn

Two hours covering the things parents actually need — not a textbook crammed into 120 minutes:

  • Infant & child CPR — practised on real-feel manikins until you've got the rhythm
  • Choking response — back blows, chest thrusts (infant), Heimlich (child) — the technique that can save a life
  • Recovery position — for both infants and toddlers
  • Fever management — when to worry, when to wait, the actual NHS thresholds
  • Febrile seizures — what to do if your child has one (more common than you think)
  • Bumps, burns & bleeds — the everyday stuff you'll actually meet
  • Allergic reactions — recognising anaphylaxis vs a regular reaction
  • When to call 111 vs 999 — practical guidance based on real scenarios

Frequently asked questions

Do you come to me?

Yes — that's the whole point. The course is taught in your living room (or NCT host's). I bring all the manikins and equipment.

What's the minimum age for baby to be there?

None — newborn is fine. Baby being asleep, awake, feeding, crying — I'm an NHS midwifery assistant, none of it phases me.

Can grandparents come?

Absolutely. I encourage it — they're often the babysitters, so they need to know this stuff too. Add them at no extra cost for couple bookings.

Is the course tailored to my baby's age?

Yes. Newborn-specific issues, weaning-specific choking risks, toddler-specific scenarios — I adjust the content. Tell me your baby's age when you book.

How do we pay?

Online via Bookwhen (Stripe) when you book. Group bookings can split the cost between couples — get in touch for the easy way to do this.

What if baby has a meltdown halfway through?

Genuinely fine. We pause. I've taught hundreds of new parents — every single class has had at least one feed/nappy/cuddle break. Built into the schedule.
